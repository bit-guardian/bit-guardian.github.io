About

I'm a Master's student in Computer Engineering at Arizona State University, graduating in December 2026. My research sits in human-centered AI: making model behavior legible to the people who rely on it, and making the human side of machine learning less costly.

Right now that means two threads — opening up large language models with sparse autoencoders to find interpretable features, and measuring the human workload behind collecting demonstrations for robot learning.

Before grad school I spent 2+ years in DevOps, running Kubernetes infrastructure in production. I also have hands-on experience across the hardware stack — RTL design, verification, and physical design — so I'm comfortable anywhere from silicon to cluster to model.
